How we work

Your camera team, for the length of a project

  • One eye from start to finish. Yann directs, shoots, edits and grades. Nothing gets lost between the set and post-production.
  • Solo or with a crew. Alone with his kit for a short format, with a camera, sound and lighting crew for a brand film.
  • Under your name if needed. We can work for the agency, in front of its client, and never approach that client afterwards.
  • Lyon, and beyond. Based in Lyon, we shoot anywhere in France and abroad.

From brief to delivery
