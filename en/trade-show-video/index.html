Trade show video in Lyon, France

Sirha, a show at Eurexpo, Global Industrie in Lyon or Paris: a few days to be seen, months of preparation behind them. We film what your stand needs to show, and what happens on it.

Before the show

The stand film, looping on your screen

A short film shot at your place before the show: your products, your craft, your workshop. It loops on the stand screen, often muted, because a trade show aisle is loud. We cut it to work without sound, with subtitles when someone speaks.

The macro stone-setting film for VJM runs 24 seconds: the kind of image that makes someone slow down in an aisle.

During the show

Vertical formats for social media

On the stand we film demos, visitors and one or two short interviews, and cut them in 9:16 for Instagram, LinkedIn or TikTok. That is what Yann did for Don Papa Rum at Winterlandia, a pop-up bar in a ski resort: on-site coverage, a 45-second vertical edit.

Turnaround is agreed in the quote: if you want to post during the show, we plan the edit around it.

After the show

The film of the event

An aftermovie serves the sales team, the website, and next year's bookings. For The Ink Factory, the Lyon international tattoo convention, Yann made a 2 min 34 aftermovie.
