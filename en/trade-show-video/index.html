Trade show video in Lyon, France

Sirha, a show at Eurexpo, Global Industrie in Lyon or Paris: a few days in the spotlight after months of preparation. We film what your stand needs to show, and what happens on it.

Deliverables · Looping stand film · 9:16 formats · Short interviews · Aftermovie · Subtitles · Stills

The stand film, looping on your screen

Shot on your premises before the show: products, craftsmanship, workshop. Show aisles are noisy, so the film works without sound. The macro stone-setting film for VJM runs 24 seconds: the kind of image that makes people stop and look.

During the show, vertical formats

Demos, visitors and short interviews, cut to 9:16 for Instagram, LinkedIn or TikTok, as Yann did for a Don Papa Rum event (45 seconds).

Afterwards, the film of the event

For your sales team, and to bring people back next year. For The Ink Factory, the Lyon International Tattoo Convention, Yann made a 2 min 34 s aftermovie. Based in Lyon, SilverFrame films at shows in Lyon, elsewhere in France and abroad.
