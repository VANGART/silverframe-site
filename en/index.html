QUESTIONS

How much does a film cost?

Every project is quoted on request, after a conversation about the brief: shooting days, crew size, number of formats to deliver. Email Yann with what you have, even if it is incomplete.

Where do you shoot?

SilverFrame is based in Lyon, France, at 99 rue de la Part-Dieu. We shoot anywhere in France and abroad.

Do you work for agencies, white label?

Yes. Yann can work under the agency's name, in front of its client, and never approach that client afterwards. M&R Digital Agency and Coming Soon Agency already work with him.

What formats do you deliver?

A 4K master, 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 cut-downs for social media, subtitles, and set photography by Auriane if needed.

Who does what on a shoot?

Yann Volle directs, shoots, edits and grades. Alone with his kit for a short format, with a camera, sound and lighting crew for a brand film.
